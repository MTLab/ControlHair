# Third-party license texts

`Apache-2.0.txt` applies to the audited upstream DWPose and DiffSynth-Studio
revisions listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and
`configs/model_sources.json`. It does not establish a project-level license for
ControlHair or override the terms of any other component.

HairStep, DiffLocks, Blender, Wan, UniAnimate-DiT, private templates, and model assets are
prepared from their official sources and retain their own terms. Their license
texts and model cards are obtained alongside those external checkouts or
downloads. See `README.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Third-party source trees are retrieved into ignored directories at setup time;
they are not included in this repository.
